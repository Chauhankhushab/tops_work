Question 4: Discuss the role of semantic HTML. Why is it important for accessibility and SEO?
Provide examples of semantic elements.

Semantic HTML refers to the use of HTML tags that convey meaning about the content they enclose, rather than just defining its appearance.

In other words, semantic elements are those that have clear and specific meaning in the context of the webpage.

For example, using a <header> tag indicates that the content within is part of the page's header, and using a <footer> tag identifies the footer section.
