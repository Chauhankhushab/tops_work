Question 1: Explain the difference between web-safe fonts and custom web fonts. Why 
might you use a web-safe font over a custom font? 


Web-Safe Fonts:
Web-safe fonts are a set of fonts that are commonly available across most operating systems and devices. These fonts are pre-installed on almost all computers and devices, meaning that they can be reliably used in websites without the need for additional resources to load.
->Pre-installed on most devices and operating systems.
->	Limited to a set of commonly available fonts.
->Free to use, no licensing concerns

Custom Web Fonts:
Custom web fonts refer to fonts that are not pre-installed on a device and need to be downloaded from a web font service or hosted on a server. These fonts allow web designers and developers to use a broader range of typefaces than what’s available by default.
->Licensing may vary; some fonts are free, others require a license.
->Unlimited choice of typefaces and styles.
->Not pre-installed; require downloading from an external source.



====================================
Branding and Unique Design: Custom fonts help create a distinctive identity for your website and align with the brand's typography.
Creative Control: Custom fonts give you access to a broader range of typefaces, allowing more control over the design and feel of the website.
